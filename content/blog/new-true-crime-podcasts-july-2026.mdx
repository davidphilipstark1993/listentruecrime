## Didn't Make the Cut This Month

Some new releases we reviewed but didn't recommend, and why:

- **Feeds that relaunched with new branding** — repackaged content from earlier seasons doesn't count as new.
- **Shows with fewer than three episodes published** — too early to score fairly.
- **Content that relies on unverified social media claims** — we don't recommend shows that haven't verified their primary sources.
