If you liked Dirty John, start with **Sweet Bobby**, **Bed of Lies: Missing Debbie** and **Love Trapped**. All three are about someone who seemed trustworthy and wasn't, told with the people who lived through it. Below are six more, grouped by the kind of lie, with who made each one and where to start.

## What made Dirty John work

[Dirty John](/podcasts/dirty-john) is a six-part LA Times podcast, reported and hosted by Christopher Goffard and made with Wondery. It came out in October 2017. It tells how John Meehan, who met Debra Newell on a dating site and married her within months, worked his way into her life and her family's. The LA Times says it was built on extensive interviews with Debra, her family and others connected to the case, plus thousands of pages of court documents and police reports.

What stays with me is that it's a family story. Goffard got the Newells to talk, and you hear them work out, slowly, that the charming man in the house was lying about almost everything. It ends with Debra's younger daughter, Terra, who fought him off when he attacked her in August 2016. Detectives told the prosecutor it looked like a clear-cut case of self-defence, and she was never charged.

So the picks below share that shape: someone charming, trust that turns out to be a lie, and the people around them working it out. When I checked the feeds on 21 November 2026, several had been re-published or were partly behind paywalls, so I've only given episode counts I could confirm.

## Online romance and catfish stories

### Sweet Bobby

[Sweet Bobby](/podcasts/sweet-bobby) is the one I'd send a Dirty John fan to first. It's a six-part Tortoise Media series from 2021, hosted and reported by Alexi Mostrous. Kirat Assi, a British Sikh woman, spent almost nine years in an online relationship with "Bobby", a cardiologist she knew of through her community. In 2018, Assi says, her cousin Simran Bhogal admitted she had been posing as Bobby all along, and as about 50 other Facebook profiles.

Assi brought a civil claim in 2020, and it settled in 2021. According to Assi's lawyer, Bhogal paid substantial damages and costs, admitted liability and gave a private apology. Bhogal had not been charged with any crime when TODAY reported on the case in October 2024. In a statement for the podcast and the Netflix documentary, she said she strongly objects to "numerous unfounded and seriously defamatory accusations".

- **Start with:** "Sliding doors", the first episode.
- **Why you'll like it:** like Debra Newell, Kirat is the heart of it, and she tells it herself.
- **Caveat:** the public feed I checked only showed a trailer and the first two episodes, so you may need the Tortoise app for the rest.

### Matched with a Predator

[Matched with a Predator](/podcasts/matched-with-a-predator) is series 4 of BBC Radio Scotland's Disclosure Investigates, released in October and November 2025. Christopher Harkins met women on dating apps, including Tinder. The BBC found that nine women reported him to Police Scotland between 2012 and 2019. He was found guilty of 19 offences, including rape and assault, admitted defrauding nine women out of more than £214,000, and was jailed for 12 years in July 2024.

- **Length:** five episodes of 24 to 39 minutes, plus a trailer.
- **Start with:** "1. The Holiday".
- **Why you'll like it:** it's the Scottish story on this list, and like Dirty John it turns on a journalist and the women who wouldn't let it go.
- **Caveat:** it covers sexual offences, so it's heavier than most of this list.

## Marriages that hid a killer

### Bed of Lies: Missing Debbie

[Bed of Lies: Missing Debbie](/podcasts/bed-of-lies-missing-debbie) is the newest pick here. It's the fourth series of The Telegraph's Bed of Lies, reported by Cara McGoogan, with six episodes released between 23 October and 7 November 2026. Debbie Cooper met Jamie Starbuck on an internet dating site and married him in April 2010. Then her sisters and friends started getting emails from a round-the-world honeymoon, with no pictures and no calls.

Starbuck admitted murdering her and was jailed for life with a minimum of 30 years in May 2013, the BBC reported. She is believed to have been killed about a week after the wedding, and he sent emails in her name for nearly three years. McGoogan tells it with Debbie's sisters, friends and the police, and it's their persistence that I keep thinking about.

- **Length:** six episodes, mostly 33 to 42 minutes, with a 57-minute finale.
- **Start with:** "Episode 1 | Missing Debbie".
- **Caveat:** the feed also carries the first three Bed of Lies series, so scroll to the October 2026 episodes.

### The Teacher's Pet

[The Teacher's Pet](/podcasts/the-teachers-pet) is The Australian's 2018 investigation by Hedley Thomas into the disappearance of Lynette Dawson from Sydney's Northern Beaches in 1982. Her body has never been found. Her husband, Chris Dawson, was convicted of her murder in 2022 and sentenced to a maximum of 24 years in prison. His appeal against the conviction was dismissed in June 2024, ABC News reported, and in June 2025 the High Court refused his bid to appeal further.

The podcast became part of the story. It was pulled in Australia while the case went through the courts, and The Australian brought it back from September 2022, with legally sensitive material edited out. When I checked on 21 November 2026, it was listed on Apple Podcasts in the UK.

- **Length:** 16 main episodes from 2018, plus later bonus episodes.
- **Start with:** "Episode 1 - Bayview".
- **Why you'll like it:** it's about a marriage that looked perfect from the outside.
- **Caveat:** it's long, and many episodes run over an hour.

### Over My Dead Body, season 1: Tally

[Over My Dead Body](/podcasts/over-my-dead-body) is a Wondery series that premiered on 29 January 2019. Season 1, "Tally", is hosted by Matthew Shaer and covers the 2014 killing of Florida State University law professor Daniel Markel in Tallahassee. It came out before any of the trials in the case. In September 2025, AP reported, a jury convicted Donna Adelson, his former mother-in-law, of first-degree murder, conspiracy and solicitation in his killing. On 13 October 2025 she was sentenced to life without the possibility of parole for the murder charge, with an additional 30 years for the other two counts. AP reported that she has pledged to appeal.

- **Start with:** "Tally | The Husband", the first episode.
- **Why you'll like it:** it's about a marriage that went wrong and the family around it.
- **Caveat:** the feed is now an Audible Original and is partly paywalled, and it also carries later seasons, so look for the "Tally" episodes.

## Lies about pregnancy and illness

### Love Trapped

[Love Trapped](/podcasts/love-trapped) is from iHeartPodcasts and Glass Podcasts, and started in February 2026. It follows former Bachelor star Clayton Echard after a woman claimed he had made her pregnant following an encounter in 2023, and the people who helped him prove what was really going on. In August 2026 Laura Owens pleaded guilty to two counts of fraudulent schemes and artifices, one count of perjury and one count of taking the identity of another. USA TODAY reported that the last two did not concern Echard. On 23 October 2026 she was sentenced in Arizona to 30 days in jail and seven years of probation, USA TODAY reported.

- **Length:** 18 main episodes so far, of about 32 to 70 minutes, plus bonus episodes.
- **Start with:** "America's Most Hated Bachelor | EP 1".
- **Why you'll like it:** it's the same question as Dirty John, about who you believe and why.
- **Caveat:** it's marked explicit, and the feed mixes in trailers for other iHeart shows.

### Scamanda

[Scamanda](/podcasts/scamanda) is a 2023 Lionsgate Sound series by journalist Charlie Webster, in eight chapters. Amanda Riley, a wife and mother from San Jose, California, blogged about having cancer. AP reported that she raised $105,513 after claiming she was fighting Hodgkin's lymphoma, pleaded guilty to one count of wire fraud, and in May 2022 was sentenced to five years in federal prison.

- **Length:** eight chapters of 35 minutes to nearly an hour and a half, plus bonuses.
- **Start with:** Chapter 1, "Amanda is Dying".
- **Why you'll like it:** it's about a community that was drawn in by someone they liked.
- **Caveat:** the feed has since been reused for other series, including The Lexi CON in October 2026, so scroll back to the 2023 chapters.

## Told by the survivor

### Something Was Wrong

[Something Was Wrong](/podcasts/something-was-wrong) is created and hosted by Tiffany Reese for Broken Cycle Media. The show describes itself as a docuseries about survivors' discovery, trauma and recovery from crime and abuse. Season 1 began in January 2019 with Sara, who, the show says, thought she was marrying the Christian man of her dreams until a week before their wedding, when she learned something was wrong.

- **Length:** season 1 has 13 episodes, and the feed now runs to season 26.
- **Start with:** "S1 Ep1: There Were No Red Flags".
- **Why you'll like it:** it's the closest thing here to hearing Dirty John from Debra's side, in the first person.
- **Caveat:** it's marked explicit, and each season is a different story.

## The person you trusted with your life

### Dr. Death, season 1

Wondery followed Dirty John with [Dr. Death](/podcasts/dr-death), and Rolling Stone's headline called it the Dirty John follow-up. It premiered in September 2018, hosted and reported by journalist Laura Beil. Season 1 is about neurosurgeon Christopher Duntsch, whose patients kept coming out of surgery worse off. ProPublica reported that he was convicted and sentenced to life in prison in 2017, "becoming the first doctor in the nation to meet such a fate for his practice of medicine". If medical cases are your thing, see our [best medical crime podcasts](/blog/best-medical-crime-podcasts).

- **Length:** six main episodes, plus later updates.
- **Start with:** "Three Days In Dallas".
- **Why you'll like it:** it's a different kind of trust, in a surgeon instead of a husband, but the same charm.
- **Caveat:** the feed is now an Audible Original, partly paywalled, and marked explicit, with later seasons in the same place.

## How to choose

- **A UK story:** Sweet Bobby, Matched with a Predator or Bed of Lies: Missing Debbie.
- **The shortest binge:** Matched with a Predator.
- **Still being made:** Something Was Wrong.
- **The most recent:** Bed of Lies: Missing Debbie (October 2026) and Love Trapped (2026).
- **Told by the person it happened to:** Something Was Wrong or Sweet Bobby.
- **If you liked the reporter getting the family to talk:** Bed of Lies: Missing Debbie or Sweet Bobby.
