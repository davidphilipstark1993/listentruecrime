Spotify has become one of the primary platforms for true crime podcast discovery — partly through its Parcast acquisition, partly through strong algorithmic recommendations, and partly because it's where a large proportion of the podcast-listening public already spends their time.

This guide covers the best true crime podcasts available on Spotify, including both Spotify-exclusive content and the wider catalogue of excellent shows available there.

## The Best Established Shows Available on Spotify

All of the following are freely available on Spotify alongside other podcast platforms.

### Crime Junkie — The Most Listened-To True Crime Podcast

**Crime Junkie** is the most listened-to true crime podcast in the world. Host Ashley Flowers covers murders, disappearances, and cold cases in tight 30-minute episodes, with a consistent commitment to victim advocacy.

If you're new to true crime, Crime Junkie is the best starting point available on Spotify. The format is immediately accessible, the research is reliable, and the episode length fits neatly into a commute.

[Browse Crime Junkie →](/podcasts/crime-junkie)

### Casefile — The Best Produced Narrative True Crime

**Casefile** is the most consistently excellent narrative true crime podcast available on any platform. The anonymous Australian host delivers meticulously researched cases with exceptional production quality.

It's a different experience to Crime Junkie — longer episodes, more cinematic, more emotionally demanding — but if you want the best storytelling in the genre, this is it.

[Browse Casefile →](/podcasts/casefile)

### Morbid — The Best Community-Focused Show

**Morbid**, hosted by Alaina Urquhart and Ash Kelley — both mortuary professionals — combines genuine forensic expertise with sisterly banter and dark humour. Among the fastest-growing true crime podcasts globally.

What distinguishes it: the hosts actually understand death professionally. That expertise shapes how they approach cases in ways that most true crime can't match.

[Browse Morbid →](/podcasts/morbid)

### My Favorite Murder — The Entry Point for Comedy True Crime

**My Favorite Murder** with Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark pioneered the comedy-true crime genre. Warm, funny, and surprisingly empathetic. The best starting point if you're not sure you want something heavy.

[Browse My Favorite Murder →](/podcasts/my-favorite-murder)

### Serial — Still the Best Starting Point

**Serial** is available on Spotify and remains one of the finest podcasts ever made. Season 1 — the investigation of Adnan Syed's conviction for the murder of Hae Min Lee — is required listening for anyone interested in the genre.

[Browse Serial →](/podcasts/serial)

## Spotify Originals and Parcast Content

Parcast, acquired by Spotify in 2019, produces a large catalogue of scripted true crime content. It's important to be honest about what this content is: professionally produced, accessible, and often lacking the depth of the best investigative podcasts.

Parcast produces **Serial Killers**, **Cults**, **Crime Junkie** (different show — Parcast's version), **Con Artists**, and dozens of others. These are dramatised productions that work well as casual listening but won't satisfy listeners who want original reporting.

The exception is **Swindled** — a satirical, anonymous podcast about white-collar crime that is genuinely distinctive, very funny, and surprisingly well-researched. It's available on Spotify and is one of the best fraud podcasts in the genre.

## How to Discover True Crime on Spotify

Spotify's algorithm is among the best in podcasting for recommendations. Once you've established what you like, it will surface related content effectively.

**Practical tips:**

1. **Use the "More Like This" feature.** After finishing a podcast page on Spotify, the "More Like This" section surfaces genuinely related shows.

2. **Follow your favourite hosts.** Spotify's notification system for new episodes from followed podcasts is reliable.

3. **Browse by category.** Spotify's True Crime category page is curated and regularly updated.

4. **Use ListenTrueCrime for depth.** Spotify's discovery tools are good for finding new shows, but the listings don't include detailed reviews and ratings. Use [our browse page](/browse) to understand which shows are genuinely worth your time before committing.

## The Shows Spotify's Algorithm Won't Surface

The algorithmic recommendation problem: Spotify surfaces what's popular, not necessarily what's best. Some exceptional podcasts with smaller audiences get consistently under-recommended.

**The Trail Went Cold** (cold cases, Canada) — one of the most carefully researched cold case podcasts available, consistently under-surfaced because its audience is smaller than Crime Junkie's.

**Hunting Warhead** (CBC/Vice) — one of the most important investigative podcasts made in any genre, available on Spotify but rarely recommended by the algorithm because it doesn't fit the standard true crime pattern.

**Missing & Murdered: Finding Cleo** (CBC) — exceptional Indigenous journalism that changed Canadian policy. Available on Spotify.

## Platform Comparison: Spotify vs Apple Podcasts for True Crime

| Feature | Spotify | Apple Podcasts |
|---|---|---|
| Catalogue size | Comprehensive | Comprehensive |
| Exclusive content | Parcast exclusives | Few |
| Discovery/recommendations | Excellent | Good |
| Cross-device sync | Excellent | Apple ecosystem only |
| Offline listening | Yes (free) | Yes (free) |
| User interface | Modern, intuitive | Reliable, clean |
| Show statistics | Available to creators | Available to creators |

Both platforms have essentially the same catalogue for major shows. The choice between them is largely a matter of which ecosystem you prefer.
