Casefile Presents is the podcast network from the team behind Casefile True Crime, and if you're new to it I'd start with **Ada**, **Missing Niamh** and **The Bakersfield Three**. The one thing Casefile fans should know first: most Casefile Presents series are made by other journalists and investigators, not by Casefile's anonymous host.

Below are the network's true crime series, grouped by type, with who made each one, its length, where to start and one honest caveat. I've left out two smaller series, In the Shadows and Silent Waves. I checked every feed and case detail on 20 November 2026. Some of these cases are still before the courts or the coroner, so I've kept to what reliable sources have reported.

## What Casefile Presents is

In the network's own words, as quoted by RadioInfo, Casefile Presents is a platform created by [Casefile True Crime](/podcasts/casefile-true-crime) that aims to give existing podcasts a wider audience while providing a platform for new podcast projects and talent. Apple Podcasts lists a Casefile Presents channel with 21 shows, including Casefile itself and its Polish edition.

One practical note: several feeds were re-published, so every episode can show the same recent date. That's often a re-upload date, so I haven't given release years unless another source confirms them.

## New: Ada (October 2026)

[Ada](/podcasts/casefile-presents-ada) is the newest series on the network. It's a seven-part investigation by Olivia LaVoice, a former KGET 17 News reporter who also made The Bakersfield Three. All seven episodes dropped on 26 October 2026.

On 29 March 2011, a woman's body was found in a vineyard in Arvin, California. The show says the killer seemingly went to extraordinary lengths to make sure she couldn't be identified. For years she was known only as Jane Doe, until the DNA Doe Project and the Kern County Medical Examiner's office identified her as Ada Beth Kaplan, 64, of Canyon Country, as the Los Angeles Times reported. LaVoice covered the case as a reporter and, KGET reported, helped get her identified by taking it to a nonprofit that worked with the Kern County coroner's office. KGET reported on 26 October 2026 that no one has ever been arrested and no suspect has been identified. Our explainer on [genetic genealogy in cold cases](/blog/genetic-genealogy-cold-cases) covers how that kind of identification works. What I like is that the series is as much about who Ada was, drawing on newspaper columns and personal writings she left behind.

- **Length:** seven episodes, mostly 16 to 23 minutes, with a 46-minute finale.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "The Mystery Woman in the Vineyard".
- **Caveat:** it shares a feed with The Bakersfield Three, so scroll to the October 2026 episodes. The case is unsolved, so don't expect an ending.

## Missing-person investigations

### Missing Niamh

[Missing Niamh](/podcasts/missing-niamh) is the one to pick if you want the Casefile host's own work. According to the show, he was researching what was meant to be a single episode and spent four years making a 12-part series.

Niamh Maye was 18 when she was last seen on 30 March 2002 in Jingellic, New South Wales, after picking apples in Batlow on her gap year, ABC News reported. A 2012 coronial inquiry found she died at or near Tumut, police believe she met with foul play, and NSW Police have offered a $250,000 reward.

- **Length:** 12 episodes, from 19 minutes to just over an hour.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "Niamh".
- **Caveat:** her remains had not been found when ABC reported in 2023, so this is an investigation without an ending.

### Searching for Sarah MacDiarmid

[Searching for Sarah MacDiarmid](/podcasts/casefile-presents-searching-for-sarah-macdiarmid) goes back to the night of Wednesday 11 July 1990, when Sarah vanished from Kananook railway station in Victoria. The show says blood at the scene suggested she had been attacked, and her body has never been recovered.

- **Length:** nine episodes, mostly 43 minutes to just over an hour, plus a 17-minute epilogue.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "Sarah".
- **Caveat:** the show says the police investigation continues, so it's open-ended.

### Julie's Gone

[Julie's Gone](/podcasts/casefile-presents-julies-gone) is by investigative journalist Helen Thomas. Julie Ann Garciacelay, a young American not quite 20, vanished from her inner-city Melbourne flat in July 1975. She has never been seen again and her body has not been found. An eighth episode followed in February 2026.

- **Length:** eight short episodes of 20 to 31 minutes.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "That First Night in July".
- **Caveat:** it's one of Australia's oldest cold cases and there's no resolution.

### Matty

Matty is a 10-part series narrated by Loren O'Keeffe of The Missed Foundation (formerly the Missing Persons Advocacy Network). Matthew Leveson, 20, didn't come home after a night out in Sydney in 2007. The series follows his parents, Mark and Faye, and their long fight to bring him home. His remains were found in the Royal National Park on 31 May 2017, and the coroner later made an open finding on how he died.

- **Length:** 10 episodes, 24 to 58 minutes.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "Matthew Leveson".
- **Caveat:** it goes through a trial and an inquest in detail, so it's a heavy listen. Not in our directory yet.

## Murder cases

### The Bakersfield Three

[The Bakersfield Three](/podcasts/the-bakersfield-three) is Olivia LaVoice's earlier series, and a big, sprawling investigation. Baylee Despot, Micah Holsonbake and James Kulstad knew each other and either died or went missing within two months in 2018 in Bakersfield, California. The series begins with three local mothers starting their own investigation.

Here is where things stand, as KGET has reported. In 2022 a man was convicted of the second-degree murder of Micah Holsonbake and sentenced to 30 years to life plus 56 years; he denied killing him. Baylee Despot has never been found. KGET reported in 2022 that she remains charged in connection with Holsonbake's death, but she has never been tried and is presumed innocent. James Kulstad was killed in a shooting that KGET reported was unrelated to that case.

- **Length:** 15 episodes from November 2023, plus three follow-ups in 2024 and 2025, mostly 21 to 52 minutes.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "Fight Like a Mother".
- **Caveat:** it shares a feed with Ada, and it's long.

### The Easey Street Murders

[The Easey Street Murders](/podcasts/casefile-presents-the-easey-street-murders) is reported by Helen Thomas. In January 1977, Susan Bartlett and Suzanne Armstrong were stabbed to death in their home in Easey Street, Collingwood, in inner Melbourne. Suzanne's 16-month-old son was in his cot.

This case is before the courts. A man has been charged, and in November 2025 he was committed to stand trial in the Supreme Court of Victoria, ABC News reported. He is presumed innocent. When I checked on 20 November 2026, I couldn't find a report of a trial date or verdict.

- **Length:** six episodes of 24 to 43 minutes, plus a 17-minute 2025 update.
- **Start with:** "One Summer Night".
- **Caveat:** the series was made before the committal and the case hasn't been tried, so it can't tell you how this ends.

### The Frankston Murders

Crime writer Vikki Petraitis, author of the book The Frankston Murders, made this 11-part series. In 1993, Elizabeth Stevens, 18, Debbie Fream, 22, and Natalie Russell, 17, were murdered in Frankston, Victoria. Paul Denyer was sentenced to life and given a 30-year minimum on appeal. He was refused parole in 2023, ABC News reported.

- **Length:** 11 episodes, 42 minutes to just over an hour.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "In the lead up".
- **Caveat:** episodes run close to an hour and cover the attacks in detail. Not in our directory yet.

### Dragonfly: Brett Cantor Murder Mystery

Brett Cantor, 25, was an A&R executive at Chrysalis and a partner in the Hollywood nightclub Dragonfly. The show says he was murdered in his West Hollywood apartment on 30 July 1993, calls the case unsolved and credits him with helping discover Rage Against the Machine. Variety reported his killing at the time.

- **Length:** 10 episodes of 33 minutes to an hour, plus a 2024 update.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "Welcome to the Jungle".
- **Caveat:** there's no resolution. Not in our directory yet.

### Also on the network

- **The Vanishing of Vivienne Cameron:** 10 episodes in which Vikki Petraitis revisits the Phillip Island case she co-wrote a book about with journalist Paul Daley, which Casefile itself covered as Case 80.
- **The Labyrinth:** 10 episodes on Janet Castrejon, who disappeared in 2015 while camping with her family near the Chiricahua Mountains in Arizona.

## Police and investigators

### The Detective's Dilemma

This is the one for British listeners. When 22-year-old Sian O'Callaghan went missing in Swindon, Wiltshire, in 2011, detective Steve Fulcher arrested a suspect who offered to lead him to her body and then, the show says, asked "Do you want another one?" The suspect was taxi driver Christopher Halliwell, jailed for life in 2012 for Sian's murder and convicted in 2016 of murdering Becky Godden. In 2014 Fulcher was found guilty of misconduct for not following arrest guidelines and kept his job with a final written warning, the BBC reported.

- **Length:** 10 episodes of 30 to 45 minutes.
- **Start with:** Episode 1.
- **Caveat:** the feed doesn't credit a narrator. Not in our directory yet.

### Crime Interrupted

[Crime Interrupted](/podcasts/casefile-presents-crime-interrupted) tells cases handled by the Australian Federal Police, from human trafficking to counter-terrorism, through the officers who worked them.

- **Length:** 18 episodes across three seasons, 2022 to 2025, each 47 to 74 minutes.
- **Start with:** "Operation Kitrino", the first episode.
- **Caveat:** it's made with the AFP, so you're hearing the police's side of the story.

## A death with unanswered questions

### Troubled Waters

[Troubled Waters](/podcasts/troubled-waters) is a 10-part series by private investigator Julia Robson with producer Clare McGrath. Louisa Ioannidis, 24, was found in Darebin Creek in Melbourne in 2011. Police found no suspicious circumstances, and in 2014 a coroner found her death consistent with drowning. Her brother Tass has always doubted that. In November 2025 the coroner reopened the investigation, ABC News reported.

- **Length:** 10 episodes of 22 to 39 minutes, plus two short updates.
- **Start with:** Episode 1, "Locate and identify".
- **Caveat:** the coronial investigation is open again, so the story isn't finished.

## Not strictly true crime

These are on the network too, but they're a different kind of listen. I've described them from their feeds.

- **The Unthinkable:** Vikki Petraitis and Emily Webb interview people who lived through unthinkable events, in two-part episodes.
- **What's Missing:** Loren O'Keeffe talks to families of missing Australians (2020).
- **Pseudocide:** nine stories about people faking their own deaths.
- **Suing Diddy:** Jack Laurence on a prisoner's $100 million lawsuit against Sean Combs, as the feed describes it.
- **The Invisible Hand:** Georgina Savage on rhino poaching in South Africa (2021).

## How to choose

- **The newest:** Ada.
- **The shortest binge:** Julie's Gone or Ada.
- **Missing-person cases:** Missing Niamh, Searching for Sarah MacDiarmid or Julie's Gone.
- **A case that's had a court outcome:** The Bakersfield Three, The Frankston Murders or The Detective's Dilemma.
- **A UK case:** The Detective's Dilemma.
- **The Casefile host's voice:** Missing Niamh.
