# Binge ratings log

Research-based `binge_factor` scores (1–10) for podcasts that had none. Started 16 November 2026.

**How scores are set.** They're based on what listeners actually say: whether they binge it, can't stop, finish it quickly or stick with it across episodes. Apple Podcasts star ratings are used only as a tiebreaker, because almost every show sits at 4.5–4.8. Scores stay close to the site norm of 6–7. A score of 8 or more (which puts a show on /category/binge-worthy) needs clear evidence that people listen all the way through or binge it. Every quote below was checked word for word on the source page. A podcast is marked **NEEDS MANUAL REVIEW** when there isn't enough real listener opinion.

Ratings and counts come from the Apple Podcasts US page on 16 November 2026 unless another source is named.
